Stage 1: CollectionCollect raw user intent, tool-call traces, general-path execution results and performance-cost facts
↓
Stage 2: NormalizationExtract stable business semantic structure from raw text and interactions, stripping concrete literal parameters and sensitive business values
↓
Stage 3: Intermediate RepresentationProduce a hashable, versionable structured representation (Query IR / Constraint IR / Flow IR / Param IR)
↓
Stage 4: Verification & ValidationRun strict checks on types, physical units, field catalog, tenant permissions, compute budget, approval status and semantic equivalence
↓
Stage 5: Optimization & Promotion DecisionWeigh call frequency, latency, resource cost, stability and expected benefit to judge whether specialization is worth it
↓
Stage 6: LoweringCompile into parameterized query plans, prepared statements, OSPF solver constraint templates, rule plans or controlled plug-ins
↓
Stage 7: PublishingComplete shadow verification, canary release, plan registry registration, digital signing, immutable versioning and atomic traffic switchover
↓
Stage 8: Adaptation & DeoptimizationKeep monitoring hit rate and benefit; roll back automatically on anomaly; invalidate precisely and recompile when schema, catalog or policy changes
