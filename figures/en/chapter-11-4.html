[Benders decomposition collaboration loop under a DDD architecture]
1. Benders master problem (stage-one strategic / master-plan decisions)• Decides the core skeleton variables (e.g. cross-line task allocation, batch sizes, main resource start/stop)• Maintains a cut pool and keeps absorbing the feasibility and optimality cuts fed back by subproblems• Maintains and updates the global lower bound (for a minimization problem)
↓Hands down the current master fixed solution (trial solution)
2. Benders subproblem group (stage-two per-workshop / per-scenario checks)• Solved independently and in parallel per bounded context or per random scenario under the master's fixed solution• If a subproblem is infeasible ──> extract the extreme ray / Farkas proof and construct a Benders feasibility cut• If a subproblem has an optimum ──> extract the dual multipliers, construct a Benders optimality cut and update the upper bound
↓
3. Cut validity and convergence certification guard (Cut & Convergence Guard)• Any candidate cut or scenario ordering proposed by a heuristic or learned policy must have its mathematical validity strictly verified from primal/dual information (it must never cut off the true global optimum!)• The master's upper/lower bounds (UB/LB) and the termination gap must be computed exactly by the certified subproblems!
↺ Re-inject the certified feasibility / optimality cuts
