Hard constraints
Physical and safety floor(such as equipment physical capacity ceilings, material conservation balance, shared feeding port physical limits, regulatory red lines)
Iron rule:Never violable; no ML calibration or agent adaptation may relax a hard constraint through training or prompts!
Soft constraints & objectives
Business preferences and optimization direction(such as order on-time delivery rate, less total changeover time, lower inventory backlog, balanced line load)
Mechanism:Converts into objective-function terms through OSPF SlackFunction plus objective weights / penalty coefficients, traded off by priority or by weight!
